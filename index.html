This policy describes the current offline version of Tiny Mine Empire, an Android idle mining game. It applies to the Play application com.tinymineempire.game. The development application uses a separate package and separate game progress.

Game progress on your device

The game stores coins, mining depth, upgrades, timestamps, partial mining cycles, offline rewards, milestones, drill unlocks, lifetime discoveries, Mining Stars and Rebirth count in its application-specific storage. These records let the game restore progress and calculate offline mining when you return.

The game uses a primary save, a temporary replacement and a backup. It does not upload this progress to the publisher. Saves are local text records, not an encrypted cloud-save service. Clearing the app's storage resets your local mine; uninstalling may also remove it. Android backup or device-transfer services may operate according to your device and account settings. We do not control those services or promise recovery of deleted progress.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

The current game has no sign-in, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDK or game-operated network service. The audited application does not request Internet, location, camera, microphone, advertising identifier or shared-storage permission. It does not send game progress, device identifiers or gameplay events to us through an in-game service.

Google Play, Android, your browser and your email provider may process information under their own policies. Hosting this policy on GitHub Pages may cause GitHub to process normal website access information; review GitHub's privacy statement. The policy page contains no analytics scripts, trackers, remote fonts or contact form.

Contacting support

If you choose Email Support, Android opens your selected email application. The game does not attach or send your save automatically. If you send a message, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, device model, app version and issue description. Do not include passwords, private keys or unnecessary personal information.

Support messages are handled by the publisher through the support email account for the purpose of responding to questions, resolving reported issues and maintaining related correspondence. Access is limited to people authorised to manage that mailbox. We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing pixelsinyapocket@gmail.com; we will review and act on requests in accordance with applicable law.

Deletion and choices

You can remove local progress through Android Settings → Apps → Tiny Mine Empire → Storage → Clear storage. This resets the game. There is no in-game account to delete. For support correspondence or privacy questions, email pixelsinyapocket@gmail.com. OS backup copies, if any, are managed through your device/service settings.

Changes

If future versions add advertising, analytics, accounts or online services, the publisher must review the data flows, update this policy and the Play Data Safety declaration, and provide any required disclosures before those features are released.
